# A colorful side-quest 

What colors to buy!? So many options, so many animals, and Minky, the most common stuffed animal fabric, is not that cheap. Since I'm not the most creative, I took the Pokemon route as the source of inspiration. It is popular, many different and cute monsters, so, why not just find out what the most common colors are and buys those which let me tell you, not much is out there.

So, why not build my OWN tool!? And that is where the first couple of weeks went. A simple webpage that grabbed all pokemon data, split their colors, get the most common and boom! Buy those fabrics.

That is where [this tool was born](https://lucascantos.github.io/pokemon-color-analytics/docs/). It does a log of data analysis on colors, finding the most common colors, the best combo of colors to make most pokemons, Pokemon with similar color palletes.

Gotta admit, most of the colors were boring colors, but they are very versatyle. But since I also wanted to try a few specific pokemons at some point, I picked a couple of more vibrant colors to play around.

## A journey of fluffy discovery

Let me start with my opinion on hobby in general, it is not the most enjoyable thing XD.

Don't get me wrong, finishing the project is amazing, seeing the tiny animal you just made all full and squishy, but boy-howdy, each step is not that much fun. You have to print the pattern, cut the pattern, draw the pattern on the fabric, carefully as not to make mistakes, cross-stich or add the details, cut the pattern from the fabric, sew all parts, stuff the animal and finally, attach all parts together. IT IS SO MUCH STUFF! And so much can and will go wrong, so it is frustrating on so many levels.

But not everything is bad. Cross-stitch embroidery is very fun actually. For eyes, the tutorial is very explicit that attaching felt is THE Beginner way of doing, since it is fast and reliable. I didn't have felt, so I decided to try Cross-stitching them. It is 10 times slower and results are worse (For beginners). It is a stylistic choice, that I had no choice but go for it. And I'm glad I did, as the results feel very personal and deliberate, but also more "professional".
